Why “honest”?

Cloud budget apps often show a polished number while silently treating bank sync as truth— or your spreadsheet as truth—without saying which. HonestSpend is local-first: your books are day-to-day truth, and bank exports (or optional BYOK link) keep them honest. When they diverge, we say so.

FAQ

Straight answers. More detail on the privacy page.

Do you store my bank password?

No. The freeware path uses files you download from your bank. Optional bank link uses Plaid keys you provide (BYOK)—not our vault.

Where does my data live?

On your computer, by default under %USERPROFILE%\.financial-os, or a folder you choose in Settings (including OneDrive if you point it there).

Is there a subscription? What’s the price?

No subscription. Source stays open. Official store builds are a one-time $49.99 purchase (lifetime personal) that will unlock Windows, Mac, and mobile clients. Pre-launch: 50% off through October 21, 2026 (~$24.99) via Microsoft Store promo. Optional third-party services (Plaid, xAI) are only if you bring your own keys.

What is Safe to spend?

Cash you can use without bouncing checking—after known bills, buffers, and set-asides. It’s the primary Home number in Simple mode.

Windows only?

Yes for the polished WinUI client today. The engine is local Python/API-first so other clients can follow; Mac/Linux/mobile are roadmap, not vapor promises on this page.

Who is it not for?

Not payroll, not e-file tax filing, not a replacement for your bank’s website. Households and freelancers who want honest cash control are the sweet spot.
