DESCRIPTION  (long — plain text for Store field)
------------------------------------------------
HonestSpend is a local-first money app for people who want one number they can
trust—Safe to spend—without handing a cloud company their bank password.

What you do (open rarely)
1. Import a bank CSV, OFX/QFX, or PDF statement (or optional bank link with your own Plaid keys).
2. When books don’t match the bank, one tap sets Safe to spend from the ending balance you trust.
3. Sort a short list of charges so categories, fees, and reports stay clean.
4. Close the month when the checklist is clear—then get back to life.

Why it’s different
• Honesty first: books ≠ bank is called out before busywork.
• Your PC holds the ledger (default under your user profile; optional folder / OneDrive path you choose).
• We never store bank website usernames or passwords. Download exports from your bank, then import.
• Optional bring-your-own-key features: Plaid and AI categorize only with keys you supply.
• Simple mode for daily use; Full books when you need ledgers, rules, and tax depth.

Who it’s for
Households and freelancers who want intentional cash control—not a social network for money.

Who it’s not for
Payroll, e-file tax filing, or replacing your bank’s own website.

Price
One-time purchase (lifetime personal). Pre-launch promotional pricing may apply in the Store.
Source code is available for audit; official Store builds are the commercial product.
