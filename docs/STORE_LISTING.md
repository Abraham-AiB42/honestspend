## Description (long)

HonestSpend is a **local-first liquidity app** for people who want a number they can trust—**Safe to spend**—without giving a cloud company their bank password.

**What you do (open rarely)**  
1. Import a bank CSV or OFX (or optional link with *your* Plaid keys)  
2. One tap to set Safe to spend from the bank ending balance when books differ  
3. Sort a few charges so tax and reports stay clean  
4. Close the month when the checklist is clear  

**Why it’s different**  
- **Honesty first:** books ≠ bank is called out before busywork  
- **Freeware money-in:** download exports from your bank site; we never store bank logins  
- **Your PC holds the ledger** (default folder under your user profile; optional OneDrive path)  
- **Optional BYOK:** Plaid or Grok only with keys *you* provide  

**Who it’s for**  
Households and freelancers who want Simple day-to-day use and Full books when the CPA season hits—not a social network for money.

**Not for**  
Payroll, e-file tax filing, or replacing a bank’s own website.
