              Why no Credit Karma / bureau link?
              
        
              
          Credit Karma and Equifax / Experian / TransUnion do not provide consumer APIs for apps like this.
          HonestSpend builds payoff plans and an educational score from debts you track here
          (balances, APR, 0% promos, limits, opened dates) plus history you enter.

                  Payoff strategy + opportunity cost
                  
        
                  
          If high-yield savings pays 6% and your mortgage is 2.625%, extra mortgage payments
          usually lose money — keep cash earning. HonestSpend uses your highest cash APY (or a manual hurdle)
          so cheap debt stays on minimum only; extra goes to high-APR debt or stays in yield.
